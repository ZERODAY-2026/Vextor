RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on this 1st day of November 2026, between:
LANDLORD: Mr. Rajesh Sharma, residing at Indiranagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka ("Lessor")
AND
TENANT: Ms. Ananya Verma, employed at Tech Park, Bengaluru, Karnataka ("Lessee").

1. PREMISES & TERM
1.1 The Lessor hereby leases to Lessee Apartment 402, Green Meadows, Bengaluru for a term of 11 months commencing on November 1, 2026.
1.2 Monthly Rent shall be INR 35,000, payable strictly on or before the 1st day of each calendar month.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT & FORFEITURE
2.1 The Lessee has paid an advance security deposit of INR 2,00,000 (Two Lakhs) to the Lessor.
2.2 If the Lessee defaults on rent payment by more than 3 days, or breaches any house rule, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited immediately to the Lessor at the Lessor's sole and absolute discretion without prior notice.

3. LANDLORD ENTRY & INSPECTIONS
3.1 The Lessor and his agents reserve the right to enter, inspect, and show the premises at any time without advance written notice.

4. MAINTENANCE & REPAIRS
4.1 The Lessee shall maintain the premises in tenantable condition. All repairs, including electrical, plumbing, wall seepage, and structural wear and tear, shall be borne 100% by the Lessee.

5. TERMINATION & RENEWAL
5.1 The Lessor may terminate this Agreement immediately without cause upon giving 24 hours notice.
5.2 The Lessee may not terminate prior to the completion of the 11-month term; early departure shall result in forfeiture of deposit and full liability for the remaining months' rent.
5.3 At the end of the 11 months, this lease shall automatically renew for another 11 months with an automatic rent escalation of 25% unless the Lessor elects otherwise.

6. GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION
6.1 This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be referred to sole arbitration appointed exclusively by the Lessor.
