AND

1.1 The Employee is appointed as Senior Software Engineer.

5.1 The Company may terminate the Employee's employment immediately without notice and without cause.
5.2 The Employee must provide a minimum written notice period of ninety (90) days prior to resigning; failure to serve the full notice period empowers the Company to claim three months' gross salary as liquidated damages and withhold relieving letters.

6. INDEMNITY
6.1 The Employee agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Company, its directors, and clients against any and all claims, customer losses, or software downtime arising out of any bug or error introduced by the Employee.
